Archipelagos

Privacy Policy

Effective 8 October 2026

Archipelagos doesn’t collect your data. Your notes stay in your vault, a folder of ordinary files that you choose. There is no account to create, no analytics and no tracking.

Your notes and files

Everything you make in Archipelagos — notes, handwriting, code, presentations, PDFs and their annotations — is saved as files in your vault, on your device or in your own iCloud Drive. The developer never receives, stores or sees any of it. If you keep your vault in iCloud Drive, Apple syncs it under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

No account, no tracking

Archipelagos has no sign-up and no developer server. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and it doesn’t share data with data brokers.

Looking things up

Some features ask an outside service for information, only when you use them, and send only what that request needs:

  • YouTube — when you add a YouTube video, its title, channel and length are looked up, and the video plays in an embedded YouTube player.
  • Open Library — when you add a book, its title and author are looked up to find its details and cover.
  • arXiv and doi.org — when you add a paper by link or identifier, its details are looked up, and an open-access PDF is downloaded.
  • Web pages — when you add a link to a website, that page is fetched to read its title.
  • Package registries — when you ask to install a package for code you run, it is downloaded from that language’s registry (such as PyPI).

These services receive the request and your IP address, as with any web request, under their own privacy policies.

Connections you choose

If you link a spreadsheet to Google Sheets or a report to Overleaf, Archipelagos exchanges that spreadsheet or report with the service directly from your device, using sign-in details stored in your device’s Keychain. Nothing passes through the developer. You can disconnect at any time.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer receives no payment details. Apple tells the app only whether your purchase is active.

Diagnostics

If something goes wrong, Archipelagos writes a description of the fault to a log file on your device. It is never sent anywhere. If you share it with us for support, we use it only to fix the problem.

Children

Archipelagos collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact the developer.
