Support

Send a question or report a problem and you’ll hear back within two working days. Please don’t include anything private: the conversation is public.

Where are my notes?

In your vault, a folder of ordinary files. On a Mac, choose File › Change Vault… or open Settings › Storage to see where it is, move it to iCloud Drive, or show it in Finder.

Subscriptions

Archipelagos Pro is billed by Apple. To change or cancel it, open your App Store subscriptions. On a new Mac or after reinstalling, choose Restore Purchases in Settings. If a subscription ends, everything stays readable until you renew.

Something went wrong

On a Mac, choose Help › Show Server Log and paste the relevant lines into your report. It stays on your device unless you send it.
